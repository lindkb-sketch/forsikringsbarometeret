# Markedsoversikt

The existing dashboard is retained under Oppsummering, including its comparison table, company cards, calendar, commentary and news. The landing page has a dedicated market overview. Its two filters select geography (Gruppe/Norge) and market (Totalt/PM/BM). Segment labels must be explicitly reported and source-verified. A Nordic business line is labeled as such; missing Norway/PM/BM and incomplete CR component sets are not filled from other scopes or periods.

The latest verified reporting period is selected automatically. Eligible companies are ranked by reported income converted to NOK using Norges Bank daily mid-rate averages for the exact completed quarter, then the largest five are displayed in ascending reported combined ratio. Company income definitions and rounding can differ; no claim of identical IFRS definitions is made. Missing FX explicitly prevents misleading ranking; it never blocks the underlying issuer financial import or overwrites source metrics.

Growth is in reporting currency versus the same quarter a year earlier. Three commentary blocks automatically summarize current verified growth and comparative CR/component trends, and link current-period documented general issuer commentary or special events when available. Scope-specific comments do not assume group events apply to a segment. No invented causes, stale figures or AI-generated financial values are admitted. Browser refresh follows existing five-minute data refresh. FX data are ancillary output of the existing pipeline; new data are not a new financial source.

All original summary and company-browser tests remain; new tests verify income ranking, CR sorting, missing scope/FX, rollover, source comments and actual browser filters.

## Locked reporting quarters

Every publication produces a snapshot of twelve views of the active reporting quarter, including source links, exact sorted selection, original and NOK-converted incomes, ratios, growth, comparative changes and generated text. At the first verified release of the next quarter, the previous published snapshot is locked; subsequent refreshes cannot change the archived content or digest. A reporting-quarter selector preserves explicit historical selection and defaults to the current quarter. The first archive is established with Q3 2026. Future active quarters may be partial as reports arrive; no missing data is borrowed from older periods. A skipped or reversed period is rejected, rather than silently replacing history.

## Trendbaserte markedskommentarer

De tre eksisterende overskriftene og visuelle plasseringen beholdes. Teksten bruker median vekst og medianendring i CR, ikke opptelling av selskaper, vinnere eller gjentakelse av rangeringen. Kvartal sammenlignes med HIÅ kun på overlappende aktører med samme inntektsdefinisjon. Forskjellen er ikke en sekvensiell kvartalsendring. Skade- og kostnadsendringer sammenlignes på samme tilgjengelige utvalg. Medianene er selskapsobservasjoner, ikke total markedsvekst. Rapporterte pris-, volum-, kunde- og risikodrivere lagres med rapportperiode, visning, sidetall, originalt dokument og kontrollsum. Manglende eller uklart kildegrunnlag skal ikke føre til konstruert pris/volumsplitt. Selskapsnavn beholdes bare ved dokumenterte særhendelser. Låste historiske snapshots endres ikke. Finans Norge og SSB er egne mulige markedskilder og skal ikke brukes som om de var IFRS-inntekter eller selskapsspesifikke vekstbidrag.
